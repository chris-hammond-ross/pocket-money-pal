    PRIVACY

    Your kids' chores, points and pocket money are your family's business.

    🏠 It stays at home

    Everything about your kids (names, chores, points, money, jars) lives on your family's computer. Not with us.

    🧾 We keep almost nothing

    Just your email and that you bought it. When you check from work, we pass messages straight on to home and never keep a copy.

    🚫 No ads. No tracking.

    Nobody watches what your kids tap, and nothing is ever sold. You pay once, so there's no reason to.

    🧒 No kids' accounts

    No emails or passwords for children. They tap their name on the family computer, and that's all.

    🔌 Works without us

    If we ever disappeared, it keeps working at home. Back it up or delete it whenever you like: it's yours.
