# App icon credits

The 🐷 in `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `badge-96.png` is the "pig face" (U+1F437) from [Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) 15.0, © Twitter, Inc and other contributors, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It's drawn on the app's navy background with a gold glow; the badge is its white silhouette.

The kiosk header's `../pig.svg` is the same Twemoji 15.0 pig face, unmodified, under the same licence.

The piggy bank's `../pig-body.svg` and `../coin.svg` are the "pig" (U+1F416) and "coin" (U+1FA99) from the same Twemoji 15.0, unmodified, under the same licence. The kiosk shows them together: the coin sits behind the pig's back.
